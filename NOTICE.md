## Frameworks referenced

- The Cyber and Operational Resilience Framework (CORF) belongs to the Central Bank of Kuwait. This project reproduces its structure (identifiers, structural titles and page numbers) so each checklist row can be traced to the official page, and writes its own area summaries and check wording. The official text is the CBK publication and every row links to it.
- Frameworks for later phases (SAMA, NCA, CBUAE, TDRA, QCB, NCSA, CBB, CBO, NIST, FFIEC, NYDFS, FTC, PCI SSC, CBI, CBE and the European Union) belong to their issuers. Each phase reproduces identifiers and structural titles only, with this project's own wording, and states its pinned version.

The assignment of controls to checklists, levels and profiles, the approval gate list and the architecture extension checks are this project's own analysis and not an official position of any regulator. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by any regulator.
